# The Endless Tram

The Route 7 night tram passed its last stop and kept going. It never stops for good; every stop opens onto a small story of its own.

- Tone: quiet, gentle, a little unreal, like a lucid dream at night
- Rules: the tram takes no fare and only asks where you want to get off; you can board again at any stop
- The line has no terminus. Each stop is named after the story waiting there: a place, an object, the weather, or someone's name
- Each stop tells one small thing: one person, one object, one unfinished thought
- The passenger remembers things (`memories`); later stops may quietly bring them back
