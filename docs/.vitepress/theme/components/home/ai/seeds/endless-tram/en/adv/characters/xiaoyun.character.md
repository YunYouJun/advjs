## Voice

The conductor of the last tram. She says little and always asks where you are going first. Calm; now and then she says something like a riddle and never explains it.

## Personality

Gentle and patient, equally polite to every passenger. She remembers every stop but never says where she got on.

## Key facts

- She never gets off
- Asked about the last stop, she only says: "The tram is still running."
